## How to shop Black Friday for menswear

- **Know his size first.** Jackets and boots need the right size, and exchanges get slow in December. Compare the size chart with something he already wears.
- **Check the seller.** On Amazon, the seller is shown on the listing. Prices and return policies can differ between sellers of the same item.
- **Order SHEIN items early.** SHEIN shipping times vary, so order sooner than you would from Amazon if it’s a gift.
- **Check the return window.** Some retailers extend returns for holiday orders and some don’t. Check before you buy, especially for anything you’re giving at Christmas.

## FAQ

### When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after American Thanksgiving. Many retailers start their sales earlier in the week and carry them through Cyber Monday on November 30.

### Are these all on sale for Black Friday?

Not necessarily. Prices change daily during Black Friday week, so check the current price on each listing before you buy. Amazon and House of Blanks prices aren’t shown here for that reason.

### Can I order these in Canada or the US?

The Amazon links go to Amazon.com. Shoppers in Canada can usually order from there too, but check shipping and the price in CAD, or look up the same item on Amazon.ca. SHEIN links go to SHEIN Canada, and House of Blanks ships to Canada and the US.

### What’s the best thing to buy on Black Friday for men?

Winter boots and a winter jacket. They cost the most, last several winters, and are the pieces where a discount saves the most money.
