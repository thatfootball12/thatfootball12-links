How to keep a winter outfit cheap

  • Price the size you'll actually buy. SHEIN prices can change by size. Pick your size on the listing before you add up an outfit.
  • Ignore huge "original" prices. Some listings show an original price many times the sale price. Compare the price you'd pay, not the percentage off.
  • Repeat your basics. Black joggers, white sneakers and a beanie show up across these looks. Buy those once and swap the top layer.
  • Order early for winter. SHEIN shipping times vary, so order a week or two before you need the outfit.
