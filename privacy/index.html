Privacy

Last updated September 28, 2026

The short version: there are no accounts, sign-up forms, comments or newsletters here, so this site never asks for your name, email or anything else about you. The only thing that collects data is Google Analytics, and it only switches on if you click Accept on the cookie banner. Decline and it never loads.

Your choice

Analytics cookies: Not chosen yet

You can change your mind any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. Switching to Decline stops Google Analytics straight away and deletes its cookies from your browser. Your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage, under tf12_consent), not sent anywhere, and we ask again after 12 months or if this page changes in a way that matters.

What Google Analytics collects

Only if you accept:

  • Which pages you view, and roughly how long you spend on them
  • The site or app that sent you here (the "referrer"), like TikTok or Pinterest
  • Which product links you click, and the product's name, store and (for SHEIN) listed price
  • Your device type, browser, operating system, screen size and language
  • Your approximate location (country, region and city). Google works this out from your IP address; Google says Analytics doesn't log or store the IP address itself.

To tell a returning visitor from a new one, Google Analytics saves two cookies in your browser (_ga and _ga_E3HV1TFPEN) holding a random ID. They don't contain your name or anything that identifies you directly, and they last up to two years unless you clear them or switch to Decline.

We use this to see which outfits and products people are actually into, so we know what to post more of.

Who it's shared with

Google, which runs Google Analytics for us. It's processed on Google's servers, which may be outside Canada (mostly the United States). Google explains how it handles it here: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

There are no ads on this site. The tag tells Google that advertising storage and ad personalization are switched off. We don't sell or share the data with anyone else.

How long it's kept

Google Analytics keeps visit-level data for 14 months, then deletes it automatically. Totals built from it (like "page views in September") stay in the reports after that, but they don't point back to any one visitor.

When you click a product link

Product links go to stores like Amazon, SHEIN and Moosehill (through the Awin network). Once you're on their site, their privacy policies apply, and they may set their own cookies to credit the sale. Some of these links earn us a commission; see the affiliate note at the bottom of every page.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent (the button above does that instantly). Since the site never learns who you are, we usually can't match analytics data to a person, but email us and we'll help however we can. If you're not happy with how we handled it, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information, or, in the EU or UK, your local data protection authority.
