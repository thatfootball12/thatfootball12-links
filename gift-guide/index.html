Christmas Gifts for Men 2026: 100 Gifts He'll Actually Wear

By Jesse, ThatFootball12 · Updated October 2026

Shopping for a guy is hard. Ask him what he wants and you'll get "nothing" or "socks." So we built this guide around the stuff men actually wear and use: boots, watches, knitwear, leather goods, cozy loungewear, and a few things for the guy who spends every Sunday in front of the game.

There are 100 gifts here, split by budget, from $5 stocking stuffers to splurge-worthy pieces from Timberland, Clarks, Fossil and UGG. Every pick ships to Canada from SHEIN or Amazon.ca, and every one has strong buyer reviews. Not sure where to start? Jump to the gift tips by type of guy, or go straight to your price range.

Gift tips by type of guy

The Gameday Guy

He's at the stadium, the tailgate or the couch every Sunday. Get him something warm that still looks like an outfit, not a costume.

Our picks: Timberland 6" waterproof boots, the Columbia down jacket, a cashmere or thermal beanie, polarized sunglasses for winter glare, and a plaid flannel for fantasy draft night.

The Office Guy

He dresses business casual five days a week and would love a few pieces that make it easier.

Our picks: Johnston & Murphy sneaker-sole dress shoes, the Marcello Vane half-zip, a thermal-lined cardigan, the Fossil chronograph, and the Komal's Passion leather messenger bag.

The Old-Money Guy

Quiet, classic and built to last. Think suede, cashmere and heritage brands.

Our picks: Cole Haan penny loafers, the Orient Bambino automatic, the Nautica peacoat, the Hickey Freeman cashmere scarf, and the Aran Crafts cable-knit half-zip.

The Homebody

His perfect weekend involves a robe, slippers and nowhere to be.

Our picks: UGG Ascot slippers, the Eddie Bauer plush robe, the DAVID ARCHY fleece lounge set, and a pajama set for Christmas morning.

What's a good Christmas gift for a man who has everything?

Upgrade something he already uses every day. A real leather wallet, a cashmere scarf or beanie, a proper automatic watch, or sheepskin-lined slippers all feel like a treat even if he "has one already."

What are good stocking stuffers for men under $25?

What do you get a football fan who doesn't want another jersey?

Gear that works on game day and every other day: waterproof boots, a warm down or padded jacket, a cashmere beanie, grippy sneakers for icy parking lots, or a cozy robe for Sunday afternoons. Check our Gameday Guy picks.

Do these gifts ship to Canada?

Yes. Every gift links to SHEIN Canada or Amazon.ca, with prices in Canadian dollars. Shipping times get longer in December, so order early, especially for engraved items like the tungsten ring.

How do I pick the right size?

Check his current boots or jacket for the size, then read the retailer's size chart and the latest reviews, which often say whether an item runs large or small. Gift receipts are worth asking for on clothing and footwear.
